Why It Matters

The New Reality of Job Applications

Understanding how modern hiring works is crucial for your career success

How have resumes changed?

Instead of being read only by humans, resumes are now first analyzed by machines. Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan your resume before any recruiter sees it.

Why is this especially important in tech?

In technical fields like software development, cloud engineering, data science, and DevOps, automated systems are essential due to high application volumes and specific skill requirements.

What does this mean for your resume?

Your resume is no longer just a summary of experience. It's a data-driven document that must be structured so computers can easily understand and match it to job requirements.

What do ATS systems look for?

ATS scan for specific skills, keywords, and formatting details to decide whether you match the job requirements. Missing the right keywords means you might never reach human eyes.
